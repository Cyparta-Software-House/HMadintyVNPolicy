8. Security
We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), storage of authentication tokens in the platform's secure keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), hashed passwords, access controls that limit staff access to a need-to-know basis, and regular patching of our systems. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your password confidential and tell us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access.

9. Children
The App is a professional tool available only to Vendors who are at least 18 years old (or the legal age of majority where they live). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights and choices
10.1 Deleting your account (in-app)
You can delete your account at any time directly in the App: Settings → Delete Account → Confirm. This permanently closes your account and deletes or anonymises your personal data, except records we are legally required to keep (for example, transaction and tax records). You can also request deletion by emailing support@cyparta.com from your registered address.

10.2 Other rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
Correct inaccurate or incomplete data (much of this you can do yourself in Profile);
Delete your data ("right to be forgotten");
Restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests;
Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out;
Not be subject to solely automated decisions producing legal effects, and to request human review;
Complain to your local data protection authority.
To exercise any right, email support@cyparta.com. We respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first. Exercising your rights is free and we will never discriminate against you for doing so.

10.3 California residents (CCPA/CPRA)
We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months. California residents may exercise their rights to know, delete, correct, and limit use of sensitive personal information using the contact details above.
